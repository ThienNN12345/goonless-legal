Local-first data

Habit progress, streaks, check-ins, reflections, counters, urge logs, and app preferences remain stored locally on your device unless you choose to share them.

Goonless does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

02

Safari Content Blocker

Goonless may include an optional Safari content blocker designed to reduce access to distracting or unwanted websites.

The content blocker runs through Safari on your device. Goonless does not collect your browsing history, does not see the websites you visit, and does not sell or share browsing activity.

Website blocking rules may be included with the app or updated as part of app updates. These rules are used only to help Safari block matching content on your device.

03

GoonBot

Goonless may include an AI assistant called GoonBot. If you choose to use GoonBot, the messages you send may be processed by a third-party AI service provider only to generate a response.

Do not enter sensitive personal information into GoonBot.

GoonBot is not medical advice, therapy, crisis support, or a substitute for professional help. It is intended only for general self-reflection and habit support.
